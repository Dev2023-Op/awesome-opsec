# Contributing
Contributions are much appreciated.
If you have knowledge with markdown open a pull request.
Otherwise open a issue.
We will try our best to get your contribution approved.

When you make a commit you agree to hand over your work to the CC0 universal license.

## Requirements
- The resource you add must be free, publicly accessible and legal.
- Add screenshots of the tool to the issue or pull request.
- The the commitments must be approved by the linting workflow.
